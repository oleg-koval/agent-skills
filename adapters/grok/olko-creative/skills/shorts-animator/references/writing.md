# Writing the lines

- One idea per line, 4 to 12 words. Read aloud it should take 1.5 to 4 seconds.
- Open on a hook that makes the second line necessary ("You have heard that it was said,").
- A turn two-thirds in ("But…", "And yet…", "Nobody ever…").
- Land the last line; give it `hold: 0.8` and a final wide or calm shot.
- Scripture, quotes and poems: use a public-domain translation (KJV, WEB) or their own words; keep it exact.
- Never invent a quote and attribute it to a real person.
- 20 to 45 seconds is the sweet spot for Shorts, Reels and TikTok; 60 s at most.
